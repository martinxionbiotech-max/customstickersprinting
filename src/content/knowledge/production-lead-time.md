A quoted lead time is a sum of steps, and most of them are not printing. Knowing the sequence tells you which delays are yours to control.

## The sequence

| Stage | Typical duration | Who controls it |
|---|---|---|
| Artwork check | 1 day | Supplier, needs your file |
| Proof and approval | 1–3 days | You |
| Print | 1–3 days | Supplier |
| Finishing (laminate, die, foil) | 1–3 days | Supplier |
| Packing | 1 day | Supplier |
| Shipping | 1–7 days | Carrier |

Our standard window is 7–12 working days from proof approval — not from order. That distinction is where most schedules slip.

## The two delays that are yours

**Slow approval.** A proof sitting in an inbox for four days adds four days to delivery. Nothing the printer does can recover it.

**Late artwork changes.** A revision after approval resets the proof and often the setup.

If a launch date matters, approve within 24 hours and freeze the file.

## Compressing a schedule honestly

- **Send print-ready artwork** — bleed, CMYK, outlined fonts, cut path. Each missing element costs a round trip.
- **Approve one proof, not three** — decide colour and content together.
- **Choose a simpler finish** — foil and spot UV add passes; a laminated label does not.
- **Order samples early** — a sample run is a separate short cycle; do it before the launch crunch, not during it.

Express production exists but it buys press time, not approval time.

## Shipping

Production lead time excludes transit. Domestic courier is typically 1–3 days; international express 3–7. If a delivery date is fixed, work backwards from it: shipping, then packing, then finishing, then print, then proof, then artwork.

## What this means for buyers

**The clock starts at approval, not at order.** Plan the approval into the schedule.

**Artwork quality is a lead-time variable.** A print-ready file removes a day and a round trip.

**Do not compress by skipping the sample.** It is faster to sample once than to reprint once.

**Build a buffer for the first order.** Second orders are faster because the proof and die already exist.
