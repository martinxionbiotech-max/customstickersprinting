Stickers fail at the adhesive far more often than at the print. The choice is narrower than for labels, because stickers go on unpredictable surfaces — a laptop, a window, a painted wall, a shipping box.

## The three adhesives

**Permanent acrylic** is the default: strong, durable, water-resistant, and effectively not removable without residue.

**Removable acrylic** peels cleanly for weeks or months on smooth surfaces. It is not reusable, and "removable" is a duration, not a promise.

**Outdoor-grade acrylic** adds UV and temperature resistance for glass, vehicles and equipment.

## Surface matching

| Surface | Adhesive | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Glass | Permanent or outdoor | Clean first; dust shows through clear film |
| Laptop lid (anodised aluminium) | Permanent | Textured finishes reduce contact area |
| HDPE / PP plastic | LSE adhesive | Standard glue slides |
| Powder-coated metal | LSE, test first | Texture traps air |
| Painted wall | Removable | Permanent pulls paint |
| Kraft board | Permanent | Porous; bonds fast |
| Car window | Outdoor-grade | Interior stickers fade differently |

## Removable, honestly

Removable stickers are used on retail packaging, returnable crates and promotional surfaces. Two caveats:

- **Time matters.** A removable label left in sun for a year may leave residue.
- **Surface matters more.** Removable on glass is clean; on cheap paint it can lift the paint.

If clean removal is the requirement, say the surface and the duration — the adhesive follows from those two facts.

## Clear film and adhesive

Clear stickers show everything, including the adhesive's behaviour: a slightly uneven bond reads as a bubble. Clean the surface, apply with a squeegee, and expect clear film to be less forgiving than white.

## What this means for buyers

**Permanent is the right default for packaging and products.** Removable is for surfaces the customer owns.

**Name the surface in the brief.** "Sticker for a bottle" is not enough — glass, PET and HDPE differ.

**Outdoor is a separate specification.** UV-stable film plus laminate plus outdoor adhesive; one of the three missing shortens life dramatically.

**Ask for a sample on the actual surface.** A sticker that holds on a desk may slide on a powder-coated panel.
