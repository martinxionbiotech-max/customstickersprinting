## Why vinyl

Vinyl is a plastic film, not paper. It does not absorb water, does not tear when a corner lifts, and holds its colour outdoors far longer than a paper sticker. For anything that will be rained on, washed, or handled by strangers, it is the default.

The trade-off is thickness: a vinyl sticker is noticeably thicker than paper, which is fine on a laptop and occasionally wrong on a curved, small-radius surface.

## Lamination is the durability decision

Unlaminated vinyl prints are water-resistant but scuff easily: a sticker on a water bottle will lose ink at the edges within weeks of going through a bag.

A laminate film — gloss or matte — is the layer that makes a vinyl sticker genuinely durable. It protects the print from abrasion, UV and cleaning products. If the sticker will live outdoors or be handled daily, specify lamination; it is the difference between a three-month sticker and a three-year one.

## Adhesive by surface

| Surface | Adhesive note |
|---|---|
| Glass | Standard permanent acrylic works well |
| Smooth plastic (PET, HDPE) | Low-surface-energy adhesive needed on some plastics |
| Powder-coated metal | Textured surface reduces contact area — test first |
| Painted walls | Removable adhesive if the sticker may be taken down |
| Fabric | Not recommended; use a sewn or heat-transfer alternative |

The most common vinyl sticker failure is not the vinyl — it is the wrong adhesive on a low-energy plastic.

## Cutting and shape

Die cutting follows the contour of the design. Simple shapes (circle, square, rounded rectangle) avoid a custom die and cost less at low quantities. Complex shapes are cut with a die, which is a one-off cost that makes sense at higher volumes.

Kiss cut is an alternative: the sticker stays on a backing sheet and is peeled by hand. It suits sheets of small stickers and designs that are awkward to handle individually.

## Quantity and cost

Vinyl costs more per square metre than paper, lamination adds a pass, and a custom die adds a one-off. At 50 units, simple shapes without lamination are the entry point; at 5,000, the die and the laminate barely register per unit.

## Ordering

Send the surface, the environment (indoor, outdoor, wet, cleaned regularly), the size, the quantity and the artwork. If the sticker must survive a dishwasher or a solvent wipe, say so — that changes both the film and the laminate.
