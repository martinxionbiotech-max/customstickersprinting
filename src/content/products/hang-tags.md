## A hang tag is judged in the hand

A label is read on a shelf from a metre away. A hang tag is picked up, turned over, and often kept after the product is unpacked. That changes the specification: the card has to feel substantial, the cut has to be clean on both sides, and the hole has to survive being handled.

It also means a hang tag is the cheapest piece of print a small brand can use to look established.

## Three decisions, in order

**1. Card weight.** 350 gsm is the working standard for a retail tag. 400 gsm feels more premium and holds emboss better; below 300 gsm the tag flexes and the eyelet can tear out. Kraft and recycled boards read craft rather than premium.

**2. Shape and hole.** A rectangle with rounded corners is the cheapest and the safest. A custom die-cut shape needs a die, which pays off above a few hundred units. The hole position matters more than buyers expect: 8–10 mm from the top edge, centred, with 3 mm of clear space around it.

**3. Finish and attachment.** Matte lamination is the default: it hides fingerprints and takes a pen. Gloss suits bright colour. Foil and emboss are for the top of a range. Attachment — cotton string, waxed cord, elastic, or a safety pin — is chosen by garment type, not by taste.

## Sizes

| Format | Size (mm) | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| Small | 50 × 90 | Jewellery, accessories, small candles |
| Standard | 55 × 90 | Apparel, socks, accessories |
| Large | 60 × 100 | Candles, home fragrance, gifts |
| Landscape | 90 × 50 | Short brand statements, care tags |
| Statement | 100 × 150 | Press kits, limited editions |

Anything outside these is a custom size and is quoted from a die line. Sizes in inches convert at 25.4 mm per inch; a 2 × 3.5 in tag is 51 × 89 mm.

## Materials

| Material | Feel | Best for | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| 350 gsm coated | Firm, smooth | Most retail tags | Standard choice |
| 400 gsm coated | Heavy, premium | Foil, emboss, luxury | Costs more; needs a bigger hole clearance |
| 300 gsm kraft | Natural, matte | Craft, coffee, soap | Colour prints duller |
| Recycled board | Textured, flecked | Sustainable positioning | Shade varies between batches |

Card is measured in gsm (grams per square metre), not microns. A 350 gsm board is roughly 0.35 mm thick; 400 gsm about 0.42 mm.

## Finishes and hardware

| Option | What it adds | Watch out for |
|---|---|---|
| Matte laminate | Fingerprint resistance, pen-friendly | Slight colour shift |
| Gloss laminate | Colour pop | Shows handling marks |
| Spot UV | Gloss highlight on matte | Needs a screen; volume technique |
| Foil | Metallic brand element | Die; minimum stroke about 0.3 mm |
| Emboss | Raised logo or border | Needs 350 gsm or heavier |
| Eyelet | Metal ring protecting the hole | Colour choice: brass, silver, black |
| String or elastic | Attachment | Knot adds 10–15 mm above the tag |

An eyelet is not decoration. Without one, a 4 mm hole in 350 gsm card will ovalise after a few weeks of handling, and the tag hangs crooked.

## Artwork

- **Bleed 3 mm** on every side; a full-bleed background must extend into it.
- **Safe area 3 mm** inside the trim, and 5 mm around the hole.
- **Hole and cut path** on a separate vector layer — not drawn as part of the artwork.
- **Two-sided printing** needs both faces in the file, marked front and back; artwork that only exists on one side is the most common omission on a first tag order.
- **Barcode or QR**, if the retailer needs one, at 100% magnification with its quiet zone intact.

## Quantity and cost

Hang tags carry the same fixed-cost structure as labels: file preparation, a die if the shape is custom, proofing and make-ready. At 50 units the setup is most of the price; at 1,000 it is a small share.

Two ways to lower the unit price without ordering more:

- **Use a standard size and rounded corners** instead of a custom die.
- **Combine two or three designs in one order**, where the card and size match — one setup, several SKUs.

## Ordering

Tell us the size, the card weight, whether the tag is one or two sides, the hole position, and how it attaches. If you have a die line, send it; if not, a sketch with dimensions is enough for a quotation.

A physical sample is worth taking on a first order: it is the only way to judge whether 350 gsm feels right in the hand, and whether the string sits where you want it.
